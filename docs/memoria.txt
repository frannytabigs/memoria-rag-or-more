SERVICE: Request for Burial Space
- Office: Cemetery Affair Section (under DGS)
- Type: Government to Citizen
- Who May Avail: Mandaue City residents only
- Fees: None
- Total Processing Time: 1 day and 30 minutes
- Requirements: 1. Death Certificate (from City Health Office), 2. Barangay Certification (from Applicant’s Barangay), 3. Exhumation and Reburial Permit (from City Health Office).
- PROCESS: 1) Submit letter request with complete requirements. 2) Section Head reviews documents (5 minutes). 3) Burial space availability check (20 minutes). 4) Certification processing (up to 8 hours). 5) Release of certification (5 minutes).
